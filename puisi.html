To My Favorite Person ♡

They say sunflowers are born to face the sun, following its light until the day is done. But I choose a different way to grow, for I found my sunshine in you.

So let the world believe I'm facing the wrong way, for I have my own reason to bloom each day. And if other flowers need the sun to shine, then let me be the light that makes you shine, as you are the light that makes me bloom.


i love you ♡
